Smarter for IT Leadership

8

About 2 minutes. For the head of custom programming. Most of what teams hand-build today for AI applications is the same plumbing: retrieval, tool calling, credential handling, safety filters and agent coordination. In Smarter that plumbing is platform functionality, configured rather than coded, and tested once for everyone. Your developers’ time moves to the work that is specific to your organization, and to reviewing manifests written by trained business staff, much as they review pull requests today. Note that the platform executes no model-generated code: tools are declared, typed calls, which is also a security property.
