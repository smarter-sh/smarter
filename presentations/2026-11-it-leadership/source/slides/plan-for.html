Maturity, and the gaps to plan for

Where it stands

  • Open source since 2024; release v0.29, pre-1.0
  • Semantic-versioned releases and changelogs
  • Automated tests in CI, with published coverage
  • Container images patched at least monthly
  • Full documentation at docs.smarter.sh
  • In teaching use at UBC

Plan for

  • Enterprise SSO: Google and GitHub sign-in today; SAML or OIDC needs integration work
  • Cloud: automated provisioning is AWS only
  • Support: community and partners; no vendor SLA
  • Perimeter: WAF, storage encryption and malware scanning come from your cloud

Smarter for IT Leadership

14

About 2 minutes. Say this before they ask; it builds credibility. Smarter is a real, actively maintained platform, but it is pre-1.0 and community supported. Four things an enterprise should plan for. First, identity: sign-in today is Google or GitHub OAuth plus local accounts. Single sign-on with Okta, Entra ID or another SAML or OIDC provider requires integration work; the authentication backends are configurable, so it is an engineering task rather than a redesign. Second, the Terraform automation targets AWS; on another cloud you supply Kubernetes, DNS and certificates yourself. Third, there is no vendor support desk or SLA; support comes from GitHub, the documentation and hosting partners. Fourth, Smarter relies on your cloud for perimeter controls such as WAF, volume encryption and malware scanning.
