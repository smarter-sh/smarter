12

About 2 minutes. This answers the data-residency question before it is asked. Commercial vendors are onboarded through a Provider resource, and their models are verified against Smarter’s feature set before teams can use them. For workloads that cannot leave your network, LLMHost registers open models you run yourself, on vLLM, Ollama or another inference stack, with the same governance: health monitoring, cost tracking and secrets handling. Because applications talk to an LLMClient rather than to a vendor, moving a workload between a commercial and a self-hosted model is a configuration change, not a rewrite. Mention that the Provider app’s self-onboarding features are still marked as under active development; the five established providers are stable.
