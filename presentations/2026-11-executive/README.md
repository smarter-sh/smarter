# The Smarter Project (executive deck)

**Audience:** senior decision makers in large organizations and educational
institutions. Most are not technical, and even the technical ones care more
about the problem Smarter solves than about its technology.

**Presenter:** Lawrence McDaniel, November 2026. 17 slides, about 30 minutes.

**The argument:** AI is already inside every organization, and two gaps stand
between it and value: people don't understand AI (the literacy gap), and
leaders can't see or govern it (the control gap). Smarter closes both, in that
order:

1. **Phase 1, literacy.** Smarter is an instructional platform. Staff learn AI
   by building working assistants, using 21 free course designs.
2. **Phase 2, production.** The same people build real solutions on the same
   platform, with no rewrite, and with guardrails, budgets and audit built in.
3. **The result, an ERP for AI resources.** One system of record for every
   AI resource: "anything an organization must own, approve, pay for or answer
   for in order to use AI."

The deck closes with why open source matters and a 12-week pilot.
