Yours

Runs on your own computers or cloud. Your data stays where you put it.

A shared cloud installation costs around US$350 a month to run. A laptop installation costs nothing.

The Smarter Project

15

About 2 minutes. Address cost and risk directly; this audience will ask. Smarter itself is free under an open-source license, for organizations, instructors and students alike. The real cost is the AI vendors’ per-use charges, which Smarter’s budgets cap and its records report. Running your own instance avoids handing your data to another intermediary, and supporting many vendors means you are not locked in when prices or policies change. Be honest about the trade-off: Smarter is community supported, with no vendor support desk, so an organization needs either in-house IT capacity or a partner to run it.
