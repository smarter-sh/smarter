The Smarter Project

12

About 3 minutes. Slow down here; this is the idea the audience is least likely to have heard before. “AI resource” is the Smarter Project’s term. Today most organizations think of AI as one thing, a chatbot subscription. In practice, using AI responsibly involves many separate things, each of which someone has to approve and pay for: the vendors whose models you use, the passwords to your systems, the data the AI may read, the actions it may take, the safety rules it must follow, the spending limits, the assistants themselves, and the record of every conversation. Walk through the chips briefly. Then the key point: when each of these is a named item with an owner and a history, you can manage AI the way you already manage money, people and assets.
