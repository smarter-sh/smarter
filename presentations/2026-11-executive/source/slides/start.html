Getting started

A practical first step

[Weeks 1–4]

Pilot cohort

[20–30] staff from several departments take one course on a shared installation.

[Weeks 5–10]

First real solution

The cohort builds one assistant for a problem they chose, and puts it in front of real users.

[Week 12]

Governance review

Leadership reviews actual costs, usage and the audit trail, and decides how far to scale.

The Smarter Project

16

About 2 minutes. End with something small and concrete. The pilot follows the same arc as the talk: literacy, then production, then governance, in about a quarter. The decision at week 12 is made with the organization’s own data rather than a vendor’s projections. Adjust the cohort size and timing to the audience. This is also the natural place to invite questions.
