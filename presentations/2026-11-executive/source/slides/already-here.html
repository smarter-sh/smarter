The situation

AI is already inside your organization

Staff use public AI tools with work information, often on personal accounts.

Departments buy AI features one vendor at a time, each with its own rules.

Few leaders can say what it all costs, what data it saw, or what it said.

The Smarter Project

2

About 2 minutes. Start from the room's reality, not from the product. Ask: how many of you are confident you know every AI tool your staff used this week? Usually nobody raises a hand. AI did not arrive through a procurement process. It arrived through browsers and phones. That creates value, and it creates risk: information leaving the organization, inconsistent quality, duplicated spending, and no record of what was asked or answered. Avoid quoting survey numbers unless you have a source you can name.
