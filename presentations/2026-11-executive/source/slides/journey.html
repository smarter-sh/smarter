The Smarter Project

5

About 2 minutes. This is the map for the rest of the talk. Where an organization starts depends on its AI literacy. If employees know little about AI and nothing about Smarter, Smarter is first an instructional platform. Once they are literate, they use the same platform to build production solutions. As those solutions accumulate, Smarter becomes the place where the organization keeps track of all of it: an ERP for AI resources. I’ll explain that term in a few minutes. For now, the point is that this is one platform and one continuous path, not three purchases.
