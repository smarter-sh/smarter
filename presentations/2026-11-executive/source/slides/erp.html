About 2 minutes. Use the analogy the audience already knows. Nobody in this room would run finance on spreadsheets emailed between departments, but that is roughly how most organizations run AI today. An ERP gives finance, HR and procurement one system of record. Smarter does the same for AI: approved vendors, budgets, permissions, an inventory of everything that has been built, and an audit trail. The difference from buying a governance product separately is that the records exist because the work happens on the platform, not because someone filled in a form afterwards.
