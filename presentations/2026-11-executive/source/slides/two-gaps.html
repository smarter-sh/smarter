The problem

Two gaps stand between you and value

The literacy gap

Most people don’t yet know what AI can do, what it can’t, or how to use it safely with real information.

The control gap

Leadership can’t see which AI is in use, who owns it, what data it touches, or what it costs.

Smarter closes both gaps, in that order.

The Smarter Project

3

About 2 minutes. Name the two gaps clearly, because the rest of the talk is organized around them. The literacy gap is about people: they either avoid AI or use it naively. The control gap is about the institution: there is no system of record for AI. The order matters. Most organizations try to close the control gap first, with policies and bans, before their people understand what they are governing. Policies written that way tend to be either too loose or too restrictive. Smarter starts with literacy and arrives at control.
