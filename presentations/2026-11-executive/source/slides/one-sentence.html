Smarter, in one sentence

A free, open-source platform where your people learn to build AI applications, and then build the real ones, on infrastructure you own and govern.

The Smarter Project

4

About 1 minute. Read the sentence, then pause. Three phrases carry it. “Learn to build”: Smarter is a teaching platform. “Then build the real ones”: the same platform runs production work, so nothing learned is thrown away. “Infrastructure you own and govern”: it runs on your computers or your cloud account, not someone else’s service.
