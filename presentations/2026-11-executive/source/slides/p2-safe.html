Safe by default, not by policy memo

Guardrails check every message, in and out: personal data, leaked secrets, manipulation attempts, invented citations, harmful content.

Spending caps for every person, team and application.

The Smarter Project

11

About 2 minutes. This is the slide for the risk and compliance people in the room. The key idea is that protections are built into the platform, so every assistant gets them automatically, whoever built it. A policy memo asks people to remember the rules; Smarter enforces them on every message. Guardrails screen what goes to the AI and what comes back. Credentials to your databases and systems are stored separately and never given to the AI model. Budgets stop runaway spending. And the record of every conversation is what makes the next section, governance, possible.
