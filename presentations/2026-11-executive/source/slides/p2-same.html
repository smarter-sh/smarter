The Smarter Project

9

About 2 minutes. This is the hinge of the talk. In most organizations, training and production are separate worlds: people learn on one tool, and then IT builds the real thing on another, usually from scratch. In Smarter they are the same platform. The assistant a learner builds as an exercise is the same artifact that goes live. That means the people who understand the business problem can carry their own solution into production, with IT governing the platform rather than rebuilding every idea.
