The Smarter Project in Five Minutes
-----------------------------------

A non-technical introduction to Smarter, for anyone deciding whether it fits their organization or
their classroom. It explains the problems Smarter solves: connecting AI to the systems an
organization already runs, governing what that AI can do, and managing which people in a
workgroup can use which AI resources.
