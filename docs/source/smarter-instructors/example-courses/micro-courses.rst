Micro Courses
=============

Each micro course teaches one subject in a short, focused course. Its page says why Smarter suits
the subject, where to look in the code base and the documentation, and suggests exercises. Each
also has three complete course plans, for a one-day classroom bootcamp, a two-week online course
and a four-week online course, with schedules, graded assignments and rubrics, capstone projects
and reading lists. :doc:`micro-courses/course-formats` explains what the three formats have
in common. They are starting points: pick, combine and adapt the ones that fit your students.
