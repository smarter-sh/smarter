- **Documentation:** the `Claude Code documentation <https://code.claude.com/docs>`__, including its
  pages on memory (``CLAUDE.md``), skills, hooks and subagents, and the open `Agent Skills
  <https://agentskills.io/>`__ format.
- **Essays:** Anthropic's `Building effective agents
  <https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents>`__, and its engineering blog's
  posts on agentic coding best practices and context engineering.
- **Research:** METR's study *Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source
  Developer Productivity* (2025), a randomized trial whose results surprised its authors, and a good
  starting point for a discussion of how to measure productivity honestly.
- **YouTube:** Andrej Karpathy's talk *Software Is Changing (Again)* (Y Combinator AI Startup
  School, 2025), and the talks on coding agents on the `AI Engineer
  <https://www.youtube.com/@aiDotEngineer>`__ channel.
- **Podcasts:** `Latent Space <https://www.latent.space/podcast>`__, and `The Pragmatic Engineer
  <https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/podcast>`__ podcast and newsletter by Gergely Orosz,
  which reports on how engineering teams actually use AI.
- **Writing:** Simon Willison's `weblog <https://simonwillison.net/>`__, which documents his own use
  of coding agents in detail, and the essays on `blog.smarter.sh <https://blog.smarter.sh>`__ about
  building Smarter with agents.
