Every lecture, lab and assignment in this course rests on the files and commits below. Every
reference is to the ``main`` branch of `github.com/smarter-sh/smarter
<https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter>`__, unless another repository is named, so that students
can open each one during class. Python paths are under ``smarter/smarter/``. The case that runs
through the course is the web console's Doodle, the illustration on the sign-in page that two
Smarter LLMClients choose and draw each day, described in `How Two Smarter LLMClients Create the
Sign-in Page's Daily Doodle <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/11/04/smarter-doodle-llm-editor-artist/>`__
and in :doc:`/smarter-framework/developer-reference/react-integration/doodle`.

The Feature Without the AI
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 45 35

   * - Topic
     - What Smarter does
     - Where to look
   * - The app's map
     - The Doodle is an ordinary Django app. Its package docstring lists each module: regions,
       schedules, the catalog, the planner, the editor, the sanitizer, caching and the api.
     - `apps/doodle/ <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/tree/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle>`__,
       `__init__.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/__init__.py>`__
   * - The catalog
     - Nineteen occasions with artwork drawn and reviewed ahead of time, each a short YAML file
       and an SVG of its decorations. A date is fixed, the nth weekday of a month, or a country's
       holiday by name, worked out by the ``holidays`` package. ``themes.py`` validates every
       file before it saves any.
     - `data/themes/ <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/tree/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/data/themes>`__,
       `README.md <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/data/themes/README.md>`__,
       `schedule.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/schedule.py>`__,
       `themes.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/themes.py>`__
   * - Regions
     - The browser sends its time zone and nothing else. Smarter maps it to one of 12 regions and
       the visitor's local date. No IP address is looked up, and nothing about the visitor is
       stored.
     - `regions.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/regions.py>`__
   * - The planner
     - The calendar alone chooses each region's doodle a week ahead: the due theme of the highest
       priority, or the default. A doodle that a person chose is never replanned. This is the
       safe answer that the AI parts improve on, and fall back to.
     - `planner.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/planner.py>`__
   * - The models
     - ``DoodleArtwork`` stores an SVG under its SHA-256. ``Doodle`` is one region's doodle of one
       day, with its status, its source (calendar, default, human or LLM) and its reason.
       ``DoodleEditorRun`` keeps every prompt to the editor, its answer and its refusals.
     - `models.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/models.py>`__
   * - The schedule
     - A Celery task plans the doodles every six hours from Celery Beat, then asks the editor about
       new days. ``manage.py plan_doodles --editor`` does the same by hand.
     - `tasks.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/tasks.py>`__,
       `celerybeat.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/workers/celerybeat.py>`__,
       `plan_doodles.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/management/commands/plan_doodles.py>`__

AI Resources as Configuration
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 45 35

   * - Topic
     - What Smarter does
     - Where to look
   * - The editor
     - An LLMClient manifest: a model, a temperature of 0.2, a long system prompt that describes
       the request and the answer, example prompts that are real requests, and two output
       Guardrails. Its taste is configuration, changed by editing YAML, not by a release.
       :doc:`/smarter-resources/smarter-llmclient`.
     - `llmclient-smarter-doodle-editor.yaml <https://github.com/smarter-sh/example-manifests/blob/main/smarter/llm-clients/llmclient-smarter-doodle-editor.yaml>`__
       (example-manifests)
   * - The artist
     - A second LLMClient, at temperature 0.6, whose system prompt describes the base scene in
       coordinates, the palette, a limit on shapes, and what it must never draw.
     - `llmclient-smarter-doodle-artist.yaml <https://github.com/smarter-sh/example-manifests/blob/main/smarter/llm-clients/llmclient-smarter-doodle-artist.yaml>`__
       (example-manifests),
       `base.svg <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/static/doodle/base.svg>`__
   * - Output Guardrails
     - The editor's answer passes OpenAI's moderation model and a Guardrail that strips a stray
       code fence so the JSON parses. :doc:`/smarter-resources/smarter-guardrail`.
     - `json-code-fence-output.yaml <https://github.com/smarter-sh/example-manifests/blob/main/smarter/guardrails/json-code-fence-output.yaml>`__,
       `moderation-output.yaml <https://github.com/smarter-sh/example-manifests/blob/main/smarter/guardrails/moderation-output.yaml>`__
       (example-manifests)
   * - Staff only
     - ``staffOnly: true`` hides an LLMClient from everyone but the platform's staff, who can
       still open it in the workbench and try it by hand.
     - `llmclient-smarter-doodle-editor.yaml <https://github.com/smarter-sh/example-manifests/blob/main/smarter/llm-clients/llmclient-smarter-doodle-editor.yaml>`__
       (example-manifests)
   * - Prompting from code
     - The platform prompts its own LLMClients through the chat api, as any client would.
       ``single_use_api_key()`` creates an api key and always deletes it, ``prompt_llmclient()``
       sends one prompt in a new session and validates the result, and ``assistant_text()``
       returns the answer. The journal and the account's cost accounting see every prompt.
     - `prompting.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/llmclient/prompting.py>`__
   * - Extracted, not copied
     - ``verify_stackademy`` had the same logic first. When the Doodle needed it, it was moved to
       ``prompting.py`` and both use it. :doc:`ADR 033 </adr/033-dry>`.
     - `verify_stackademy.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/llmclient/management/commands/verify_stackademy.py>`__
   * - Finding the resources
     - ``smarter_llmclient()`` returns the editor or the artist, or ``None`` when the example
       manifests aren't loaded, in which case the calendar does the whole job.
     - `editor.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/editor.py>`__

Trust Nothing the Model Says
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 45 35

   * - Topic
     - What Smarter does
     - Where to look
   * - A menu, not a blank page
     - ``editor_request()`` builds a JSON document that lists, for each region, exactly what the
       editor may choose: the catalog's due themes, the region's other holidays, and its trends.
       ``default`` is always a safe choice.
     - `editor.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/editor.py>`__
   * - The contract with the model
     - Pydantic models describe the request (``DoodleEditorRequest``) and the answer
       (``DoodleEditorAnswer``, ``DoodleEditorDecision``), with a ``reason`` for every choice.
       ``parse_answer()`` refuses anything that doesn't validate. :doc:`ADR 031
       </adr/031-pydantic-over-json>`.
     - `editor.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/editor.py>`__
   * - Every choice checked again
     - ``check_decision()`` enforces in code what the system prompt asks: the choice must be on
       the region's menu, of an allowed category, with a short caption and alt text, and a
       Wikipedia link or none. A refused choice is recorded, and the region keeps the calendar's
       doodle.
     - `editor.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/editor.py>`__
   * - Blocked topics
     - ``has_blocked_word()`` refuses mourning, war, politics, accidents, crimes and the like, with
       the same language matching as the Plugins' selectors: plurals, typos and WordNet synonyms,
       never a word inside another.
     - `editor.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/editor.py>`__,
       `nlp.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/llmtool/nlp.py>`__
   * - Inputs filtered before the model
     - Trends come from Wikipedia's most viewed articles. ``region_trends()`` drops people, dates
       and blocked words, and keeps only articles whose English description names an occasion,
       an allowlist. They are fetched with the Plugins' hardened HTTP client and cached for 12
       hours.
     - `trends.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/trends.py>`__,
       `safe_http.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/llmtool/plugin/safe_http.py>`__

Judges, Sanitizers and People
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 45 35

   * - Topic
     - What Smarter does
     - Where to look
   * - A judge of tone
     - Word lists can't tell sad news from a festival. ``upbeat_occasion_input`` is an
       ``llm_judge`` Guardrail that asks a small model whether an occasion is plainly cheerful.
     - `upbeat-occasion-input.yaml <https://github.com/smarter-sh/example-manifests/blob/main/smarter/guardrails/upbeat-occasion-input.yaml>`__
       (example-manifests),
       `llm_judge_strategy.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/guardrail/services/strategies/llm_judge_strategy.py>`__
   * - Failing closed
     - ``tone_check()`` declines an occasion when the judge says no, when it fails, and when the
       Guardrail doesn't exist on the platform.
     - `editor.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/editor.py>`__
   * - Asking a Guardrail directly
     - ``evaluate()`` runs one Guardrail on one text and returns whether it triggered, without a
       chat. ``tone_check()`` calls it in-process, and the Guardrail api's evaluate view calls it
       over HTTP.
     - `guardrail/api/v1/views/views.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/guardrail/api/v1/views/views.py>`__
   * - Models that differ
     - A model that rejects a parameter, as one rejects the judge's temperature, is retried once
       without it. The chat provider and the judge share one list of such quirks.
     - `model_quirks.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/lib/openai/model_quirks.py>`__,
       `clients.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/guardrail/services/strategies/clients.py>`__
   * - Generated markup
     - The artist's SVG is untrusted. The sanitizer keeps an allowlist of shapes and attributes,
       strips handlers, links, ids, ``url()``, ``javascript:`` and ``data:``, limits CSS to the
       drawing's own prefixed classes, and caps its size.
     - `sanitize.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/sanitize.py>`__,
       `artwork.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/artwork.py>`__
   * - Two locks on one door
     - The page shows artwork with ``<img>``, in which a browser runs no scripts, and the artwork's
       url sends a sandboxing Content-Security-Policy and ``nosniff``.
     - `views.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/api/v1/views.py>`__
   * - A person decides
     - A drawing is saved as proposed. In the Django admin, *Preview the week* shows every
       region's doodle for every planned day, with who chose it and the editor's reason, and
       Approve and Reject buttons. Doodle Editor Runs are the paper trail.
     - `admin.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/admin.py>`__
   * - Switches
     - The editor, the artwork, the trends and the credit each have a waffle switch.
       ``enable_doodle_auto_approve``, which skips review, is off by default.
     - `switches.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/lib/django/waffle/switches.py>`__

Into the Product
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 45 35

   * - Topic
     - What Smarter does
     - Where to look
   * - The api
     - ``GET /api/v1/doodles/today/?tz=`` returns the day's doodle as a Pydantic contract, with
       ``aiCredit`` and ``learnHowUrl``. Caching falls back from the region's approved doodle to
       the calendar's choice, so a drawing that awaits approval never leaves a gap.
     - `contract/models.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/contract/models.py>`__,
       `caching.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/caching.py>`__
   * - The page
     - The single-page console's sign-in pages show the doodle, its caption, and "Chosen by" or
       "Drawn by a Smarter AI application" with a link that explains how; until release v0.42.10, a
       React app that Django hosted showed it, with a template tag.
       :doc:`/smarter-framework/developer-reference/react-integration`.
     - `smarter-doodle/src/App.tsx <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/react/packages/smarter-doodle/src/App.tsx>`__,
       `AuthCard.tsx <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/react/packages/smarter-console/src/components/AuthCard.tsx>`__,
       `react_doodle.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/v0.42.10/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/templatetags/react_doodle.py>`__
   * - Testing with the models patched
     - The editor's and artist's answers are patched in tests, with a test for every refusal and
       every failure. The app has well over a hundred tests.
     - `test_editor.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/tests/test_editor.py>`__
   * - Dangerous input on file
     - The sanitizer is tested against saved drawings, one safe, one crowded, one empty and one
       dangerous; trends against saved Wikimedia responses, so no test needs the network.
     - `tests/data/ <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/tree/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/tests/data>`__,
       `test_sanitize.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/tests/test_sanitize.py>`__,
       `test_trends.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/doodle/tests/test_trends.py>`__

The Doodle in the History
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Doodle was built in three phases, each a commit whose body explains what it adds, how it was
tested and what it ran into, so students can watch an AI feature grow from a calendar.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 45 35

   * - Commit
     - Why it is worth studying
     - Where to look
   * - Phase 1: a calendar
     - The whole feature with no LLM at all: regions, schedules, the catalog, the planner, the api,
       the React app and the switches. The safe answer comes first.
     - `commit 0df9280ff <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/commit/0df9280ff18c1155b6d1b8111a35dd42ec6d3302>`__
   * - Phase 2: an editor and an artist
     - Two LLMClients, the prompting helpers extracted from ``verify_stackademy``, the checks, the
       sanitizer, the editor runs and the admin's Approve and Reject. Its body records what the
       models did when they were run for real.
     - `commit d07b91f67 <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/commit/d07b91f677ff0b8428ac36c62be9b5670f7492b1>`__
   * - Phase 3: trends and a judge
     - Wikipedia trends, the tone check that fails closed, and a fix to every ``llm_judge``
       Guardrail on a model that rejected its temperature.
     - `commit 2e6cb0806 <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/commit/2e6cb0806c126bcdf064f278d785f2901665aaa8>`__
   * - Credit where it's due
     - The "Learn how" link under an AI-chosen doodle points to the article that explains it.
     - `commit f00ac5cac <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/commit/f00ac5cac5fb7d16859c239a9cbae491c428c6c7>`__
