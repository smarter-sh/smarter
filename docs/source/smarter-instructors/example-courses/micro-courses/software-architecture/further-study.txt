- **Methods:** Simon Brown's `C4 model <https://c4model.com/>`__ for diagrams, the `ADR community
  site <https://adr.github.io/>`__, and Michael Nygard's original post, `Documenting Architecture
  Decisions <https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions>`__ (2011).
- **Principles:** `The Twelve-Factor App <https://12factor.net/>`__, and Martin Fowler's writing on
  architecture, microservices and evolutionary design at `martinfowler.com
  <https://martinfowler.com/architecture/>`__.
- **Patterns:** Martin Fowler, `GUI Architectures <https://martinfowler.com/eaaDev/uiArchs.html>`__,
  on Model-View-Controller and its successors, and the Django FAQ's answer to why Django `calls the
  controller a view
  <https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/stable/faq/general/#django-appears-to-be-a-mvc-framework-but-you-call-the-controller-the-view-and-the-view-the-template-how-come-you-don-t-use-the-standard-names>`__.
- **Podcasts:** `Software Engineering Radio <https://se-radio.net/>`__ and the `InfoQ Podcast
  <https://www.infoq.com/podcasts/>`__, both with frequent architecture interviews.
- **YouTube:** the `GOTO Conferences <https://www.youtube.com/@GOTO->`__ channel, Mark Richards's
  `Software Architecture Monday <https://www.developertoarchitect.com/lessons/>`__ lessons, and
  Simon Brown's talks on the C4 model.
- **Case studies:** *The Architecture of Open Source Applications* (free at `aosabook.org
  <https://aosabook.org/>`__), in which the developers of well-known systems explain their designs.
- **Smarter:** articles on `blog.smarter.sh <https://blog.smarter.sh/>`__ related to this course:
  `One Image, Laptop to Production: The Case for Smarter on Docker and Kubernetes
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/04/11/smarter-docker-kubernetes/>`__, on ADR 002 and ADR 003, and
  one image from a laptop to a production cluster; `Smarter's OpenAPI Contract: One Extensibility
  Model for Python, Go, React and VS Code
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/09/05/smarter-openapi-contract/>`__, on the generated, tested
  OpenAPI document that every client depends on; `Smarter's Ownership Model: One Base Class for
  Owning, Sharing and Tracking AI Resources
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/07/18/smarter-metadata-with-ownership/>`__, on who owns, sees,
  changes and pays for each resource; `Django and AWS: Keeping the Cloud Out of Reach of Application
  Code and Tests <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/09/03/cloud-out-of-reach-of-tests/>`__, on the
  service layer and guard that keep AWS and Kubernetes out of the tests; `Why Smarter Abandoned
  MySQL & Nginx <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/05/02/why-smarter-abandoned-mysql-nginx/>`__, on what
  production taught about MariaDB, Traefik and cost; `Part 2: The Antipattern That Makes a Codebase
  Diverge <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/10/11/blog-prologue-geometric-series/>`__, on the pattern
  that makes a series of agent changes diverge, and how to find it. The case studies `Converge or
  Diverge: AI Coding Agents <https://smarter.sh/case-studies/ai-coding-agents>`__, on the
  properties of a codebase that keep a coding agent on course, `One Contract, Five Clients
  <https://smarter.sh/case-studies/api-contract>`__, on a generated API contract and its clients,
  and `What Happens When You Click Deploy <https://smarter.sh/case-studies/deploy>`__, on turning a
  manifest into DNS records, certificates and ingresses, turn these posts into lessons with labs
  and discussion questions.
