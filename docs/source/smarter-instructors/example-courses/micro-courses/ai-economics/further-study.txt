- **Price and performance data:** `Artificial Analysis <https://artificialanalysis.ai/>`__, which
  compares models on quality, speed and price, and the providers' own pricing pages.
- **Cloud cost management:** the `FinOps Foundation <https://www.finops.org/>`__, its framework, and
  its working group material on the cost of AI.
- **Reports:** the annual `AI Index <https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index>`__ from Stanford HAI, with
  chapters on the economy and on the cost of training and running models.
- **Podcasts:** `Exponential View <https://www.exponentialview.co/>`__ with Azeem Azhar, on
  technology and the economy, `Odd Lots <https://www.bloomberg.com/oddlots>`__ from Bloomberg, which
  regularly covers AI and the data center build-out, and the `a16z Podcast
  <https://a16z.com/podcasts/>`__.
- **Writing:** Ethan Mollick's `One Useful Thing <https://www.oneusefulthing.org/>`__ on AI and
  productivity at work, and Benedict Evans's `newsletter and presentations
  <https://www.ben-evans.com/>`__ on technology markets.
- **YouTube:** talks by Ajay Agrawal, Joshua Gans and Avi Goldfarb on the economics of prediction,
  and Daron Acemoglu's lectures on automation and inequality.
- **Smarter:** articles on `blog.smarter.sh <https://blog.smarter.sh/>`__ related to this course:
  `Django and httpx: An LLM Proxy That Keeps Provider API Keys Away From Callers
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/03/07/smarter-llm-proxy-api-keys/>`__, on keeping the credential
  that spends money at a provider in one place; `Why Smarter Abandoned MySQL & Nginx
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/05/02/why-smarter-abandoned-mysql-nginx/>`__, on what production
  taught about MariaDB, Traefik and cost; `Why LLMs Love Code, and What That Means for Everyone Else
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/07/09/why-llms-love-code-and-what-that-means-for-everyone-else/>`__,
  on why LLMs do better at code than at other work. The case studies `Converge or Diverge: AI
  Coding Agents <https://smarter.sh/case-studies/ai-coding-agents>`__, on what an AI coding agent
  did to a codebase's pace, and `Guardrails: Can You Trust the Model?
  <https://smarter.sh/case-studies/guardrails>`__, on what each kind of guardrail costs, turn
  these ideas into lessons with labs and discussion questions.
