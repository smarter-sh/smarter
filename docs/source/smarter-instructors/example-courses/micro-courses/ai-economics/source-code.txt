Every lecture, lab and assignment in this course measures costs with the records, prices and
manifests below. Students see the records in the web console and the dashboard. The files are on
the ``main`` branch of `github.com/smarter-sh/smarter <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter>`__,
for instructors and for students who want to see how a cost is computed. Python paths are under
``smarter/smarter/``.

Measuring Cost
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 45 35

   * - Topic
     - What Smarter does
     - Where to look
   * - The charge
     - One record for each model call, plugin call or tool call, and for each hour of
       self-hosted compute: the account, the user, the session, the provider, the model, the
       prompt, completion and total tokens, and the cost in USD. A charge is created for every
       resource that took part, so the same request is seen by the user's, the application's
       and the account's budgets. :doc:`/smarter-platform/cost-accounting`.
     - `account/models/charge.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/account/models/charge.py>`__
   * - Prices
     - A price is USD per million tokens, markup included, for each combination of charge type,
       provider and model. A model without a price costs nothing, so the instructor loads the
       prices of the class's models before Lab 1, as shown in
       :doc:`/smarter-platform/cost-accounting`.
     - `account/models/llm_prices.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/account/models/llm_prices.py>`__
       (``cost_of()``)
   * - Totals for a period
     - Charges are aggregated by hour, and summed for any resource and time range, which is what
       a budget compares with its limits.
     - `account/models/charge.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/account/models/charge.py>`__
       (``AggregatedCharges``, ``get_actuals()``)
   * - The dashboard
     - Charts of charges and budgets in the web console, served by the dashboard's API.
     - `dashboard/views/views/api/charges.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/dashboard/views/views/api/charges.py>`__,
       `budgets.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/dashboard/views/views/api/budgets.py>`__

Comparing Models and Hosting
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 45 35

   * - Topic
     - What Smarter does
     - Where to look
   * - Changing the model
     - An LLMClient's ``provider`` and ``defaultModel`` are two lines of its manifest, so the
       same tasks run on another provider's model with no other change.
     - `llmclient-simple.yaml <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/llmclient/data/llm-clients/llmclient-simple.yaml>`__
   * - Providers
     - Each LLM provider that Smarter can call is a Provider resource, and new providers that
       speak the OpenAI protocol are added with a manifest.
       :doc:`/smarter-resources/smarter-provider`.
     - `apps/provider/ <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/tree/main/smarter/smarter/apps/provider>`__
   * - The price of a GPU node
     - Seven built-in compute profiles, from small CPU nodes to eight H100s, each with its AWS
       on-demand price per hour, from which self-hosted models are charged hourly.
     - `llmhost/data/compute/ <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/tree/main/smarter/smarter/apps/llmhost/data/compute>`__,
       `gpu-l4-1x.yaml <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/llmhost/data/compute/gpu-l4-1x.yaml>`__
   * - Open models
     - Twenty-four built-in LLMHost manifests for Llama, Gemma, Qwen, Mistral, Phi, DeepSeek and
       gpt-oss models, and embedding models, to price against per-token APIs.
       :doc:`/smarter-resources/smarter-llmhost`.
     - `llmhost/data/llmhost/ <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/tree/main/smarter/smarter/apps/llmhost/data/llmhost>`__
   * - Platform infrastructure
     - The cluster, nodes, load balancer, database and other AWS services that a shared Smarter
       instance needs, all declared in Terraform, so each fixed cost can be traced to a resource.
     - `smarter-infrastructure <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter-infrastructure>`__,
       `aws/terraform/ <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter-infrastructure/tree/main/aws/terraform>`__

Controlling Spending
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 45 35

   * - Topic
     - What Smarter does
     - Where to look
   * - Budgets
     - Limits in dollars or tokens, per hour, day, week or month and in total, for an account, a
       user, an application, a provider, a proxy or a self-hosted compute profile, that block or
       warn. :doc:`/smarter-resources/smarter-budget`.
     - `account/manifest/models/budget/spec.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/account/manifest/models/budget/spec.py>`__
   * - Seven budget policies
     - A student allowance ($10 a month, $100 in total), an account-wide monthly cap, a provider
       limited to $200 a day, a six-month project budget, a GPU budget of $500 a month, a
       warn-only budget on a proxy, and a token throttle for each user.
     - `account/data/example-manifests/budgets/ <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/tree/main/smarter/smarter/apps/account/data/example-manifests/budgets>`__
   * - Warnings and limits
     - A ``budget_warning`` signal at the warning threshold, once per period, and a
       ``budget_exceeded`` signal and a lock when a limit is reached.
     - `account/signals.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/account/signals.py>`__,
       `account/models/budget.py <https://github.com/smarter-sh/smarter/blob/main/smarter/smarter/apps/account/models/budget.py>`__
