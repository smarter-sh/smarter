- **Official tutorials:** HashiCorp's `Terraform tutorials
  <https://developer.hashicorp.com/terraform/tutorials>`__, the `Terragrunt documentation
  <https://terragrunt.gruntwork.io/docs/>`__, and `OpenTofu <https://opentofu.org/docs/>`__, the
  open source fork of Terraform.
- **Architecture guidance:** the `AWS Well-Architected Framework
  <https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/well-architected/>`__, especially its security and cost
  optimization pillars, and the `Amazon EKS Best Practices Guide
  <https://docs.aws.amazon.com/eks/latest/best-practices/introduction.html>`__.
- **Tools:** `Infracost <https://www.infracost.io/>`__ for cost estimates from a plan, `Checkov
  <https://www.checkov.io/>`__ and `tflint <https://github.com/terraform-linters/tflint>`__ for
  static analysis, and `terraform-docs <https://terraform-docs.io/>`__ for module documentation.
- **Podcasts:** `Screaming in the Cloud
  <https://www.lastweekinaws.com/podcast/screaming-in-the-cloud/>`__ with Corey Quinn, on cloud
  economics and architecture, and the `AWS Podcast
  <https://aws.amazon.com/podcasts/aws-podcast/>`__.
- **YouTube:** HashiConf talks on the `HashiCorp <https://www.youtube.com/@HashiCorp>`__ channel,
  and AWS re:Invent sessions on the `AWS Events <https://www.youtube.com/@AWSEventsChannel>`__
  channel.
- **Writing:** the `Last Week in AWS <https://www.lastweekinaws.com/>`__ newsletter, and the
  `Gruntwork blog <https://blog.gruntwork.io/>`__ on Terraform and Terragrunt in practice.
- **Smarter:** articles on `blog.smarter.sh <https://blog.smarter.sh/>`__ related to this course:
  `cert-manager and Route 53: DNS-01 Certificates, One Issuer per Delegated Zone
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/10/01/cert-manager-dns01-route53/>`__, on Let's Encrypt certificates
  through the DNS-01 challenge; `Amazon SES, DKIM and DMARC: Email Authentication for Every Smarter
  Environment <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/08/09/amazon-ses-dkim-dmarc/>`__, on SES identities,
  DKIM and DMARC in Terraform, and the tradeoffs left in; `Route 53 and cert-manager: One DNS Record
  and One TLS Certificate per Deployed LLM
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/03/24/smarter-per-host-dns-tls/>`__, on per-chatbot DNS and
  certificates instead of wildcards; `Route 53 and Celery Beat: A Daily Sweep for Orphaned DNS
  Records <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/06/29/smarter-orphan-dns-sweep/>`__, on safely deleting
  dangling DNS records in production; `Django and AWS: Keeping the Cloud Out of Reach of Application
  Code and Tests <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/09/03/cloud-out-of-reach-of-tests/>`__, on the
  service layer and guard that keep AWS and Kubernetes out of the tests. The case study `What
  Happens When You Click Deploy <https://smarter.sh/case-studies/deploy>`__, on `smarter.sh
  <https://smarter.sh/case-studies>`__, brings these together, with labs and discussion questions.
