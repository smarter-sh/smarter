- **Official documentation:** the `Kubernetes tutorials <https://kubernetes.io/docs/tutorials/>`__
  and `concepts <https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/>`__, and the `Helm documentation
  <https://helm.sh/docs/>`__.
- **Hands-on:** Kelsey Hightower's `Kubernetes the Hard Way
  <https://github.com/kelseyhightower/kubernetes-the-hard-way>`__, for students who want to build a
  cluster by hand, and `kind <https://kind.sigs.k8s.io/>`__ and `minikube
  <https://minikube.sigs.k8s.io/>`__ for local clusters.
- **Certification:** the Linux Foundation's Certified Kubernetes Application Developer (CKAD) and
  Administrator (CKA) curricula, which make good study plans even without the exam.
- **Podcasts:** `The Kubernetes Podcast from Google <https://kubernetespodcast.com/>`__, and `Ship
  It! <https://changelog.com/shipit>`__ from Changelog (archived, still worth the back catalog).
- **YouTube:** KubeCon talks on the `CNCF <https://www.youtube.com/@cncf>`__ channel, and `TechWorld
  with Nana <https://www.youtube.com/@TechWorldwithNana>`__ for introductions.
- **Writing:** the `Kubernetes blog <https://kubernetes.io/blog/>`__, and Learnk8s's `articles
  <https://learnk8s.io/blog>`__, which explain internals with diagrams.
- **Smarter:** articles on `blog.smarter.sh <https://blog.smarter.sh/>`__ related to this course:
  `One Image, Laptop to Production: The Case for Smarter on Docker and Kubernetes
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/04/11/smarter-docker-kubernetes/>`__, on ADR 002 and ADR 003, and
  one image from a laptop to a production cluster; `Route 53 and cert-manager: One DNS Record and
  One TLS Certificate per Deployed LLM
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/03/24/smarter-per-host-dns-tls/>`__, on per-chatbot DNS and
  certificates instead of wildcards; `cert-manager and Route 53: DNS-01 Certificates, One Issuer per
  Delegated Zone <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/10/01/cert-manager-dns01-route53/>`__, on Let's
  Encrypt certificates through the DNS-01 challenge; `Helm, cosign and Amazon ECR: What Smarter's
  Supply Chain Verifies, and What It Takes on Trust
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/04/23/helm-cosign-supply-chain/>`__, on the signed Helm chart,
  scanned images and npm provenance, and their limits; `Why Smarter Abandoned MySQL & Nginx
  <https://blog.smarter.sh/2026/05/02/why-smarter-abandoned-mysql-nginx/>`__, on what production
  taught about MariaDB, Traefik and cost. Case studies on `smarter.sh
  <https://smarter.sh/case-studies>`__, with labs and discussion questions: `What Happens When You
  Click Deploy <https://smarter.sh/case-studies/deploy>`__, on the DNS record, Ingress and
  certificate that each deployed chatbot gets; and `Guardrails: Can You Trust the Model?
  <https://smarter.sh/case-studies/guardrails>`__, whose section "URLs a model chooses" shows the
  application refusing private addresses, such as the metadata address.
